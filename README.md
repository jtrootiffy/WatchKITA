# WatchKITA

**KITA** means "us" in Waray-Waray. **Watch** is also **KITA**. So **WatchKITA** is *KITA KITA* — **let's watch, together**.

WatchKITA is a cinematic streaming guide for series and movies. Browse the catalog, discover what's new, and see where titles are available to watch — all in one dark, editorial experience built for film and TV lovers.

## The app

WatchKITA puts movies and TV shows front and center with a theater-dark interface, bold red accents, and editorial typography.

- **Home** — rotating featured hero, trending and popular rails, recently released titles, and genre picks.
- **Movies & TV Shows** — shared catalog browser with genre filters, release-year filters, sort options, region switching, removable filter chips, and load-more pagination.
- **Discover** — explore the full catalog across types, genres, and regions.
- **Search** — full-screen search overlay with debounced results, keyboard navigation, recent searches, and a full results page.
- **Title details** — artwork, synopsis, year, runtime, content rating, audience and critic scores, trailer link, watchlist and share actions, provider availability grouped by subscription/free/rental/purchase, cast and crew, TV seasons and episodes, and similar titles.
- **Watchlist** — save titles to watch later, with grid and list views, filtering, per-item removal, and confirmed clear-all.
- **Region switching** — availability is always tied to the region you select.

## What it is

WatchKITA is a discovery guide, not a streaming service. It shows catalog metadata and where titles are legally available in your selected region. It never hosts or streams video.
